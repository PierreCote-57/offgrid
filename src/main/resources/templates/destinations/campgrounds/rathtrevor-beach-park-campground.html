Rathtrevor Beach Provincial Park

Beautiful park, standard BC Parks camping sites. The beach does stretch forever at low tide! Much description and reviews available elsewhere

Of note: Depending on your site (we were 218), you may need to go past the site turnoff to get to the gatehouse, then make a U-turn to get to your site.

As expected, you are on pavement except dirt/gravel road from the turnoff to the campig sites. Everything is well managed: The dirt road is good, there are garbage and recycling bins and clean pit toilets nearby.
