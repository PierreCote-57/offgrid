The sky above you

My God, it's full of stars! David Bowman

Most of us live in a city. Even a small city will put a dome of light above its sky, severely limiting star visibility. Add that your eyes tune themselves for the ambient light level created by modern life, it's no wonder we are used to seeing only a few stars.

Get away from the city, in one of BC's wonderful parks, look up, and let yourself be transformed through a world full of stars. The scale of it is humbling while being magnificient.
