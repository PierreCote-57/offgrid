How to: Water connections

Looking at the blue hose connection on :

  1. Hooks up to the coiled blue hose with special connector
  2. This hose is for rinsing and washing hands after emptying the dirty water tanks.
  3. Gets its water from City water if available, else van tank

Looking at , we see:

  1. Fresh water connection
    1. Incoming
    2. To fresh water tank
    3. Overflow under van
  2. Black tank flush
    1. Incoming
    2. To black water tank's clean spray
    3. WARNING: Use ONLY with black tank flush valve opened
    4. May cause overflow inside van
  3. City water connection
    1. Incoming
    2. Potable water
    3. Overrides fresh water tank
    4. Does not require water pump
