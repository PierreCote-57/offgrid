Checklist: Sewer at station

It's a dirty job, but somebody's got to do it!

Getting ready - Have on hand
  1. The sewer hose & fittings
  2. A fresh-water hose
  3. A black-water hose
  4. Disposable gloves
  5. Hand sanitizer

At the station
  1. Remove the sewer cap from the outlet.
  2. Secure one end of the sewer hose into the dump station drain, using the clear elbow.
  3. Connect the other end to the van's outlet.
  4. Validate all connections are correct and secure.
  5. Open the black water valve first and let it fully drain.
  6. Close the black water valve once it stops flowing.
  7. Open the gray water valve and let it fully drain.
  8. Close the gray water valve once it stops flowing.
  9. Open the black water valve.
  10. Connect the black-water hose to the black tank flush port.
  11. Turn on the water, run it until the flow into the drain runs clear.
  12. Turn off the water.
  13. Disconnect the black-water hose from the flush port.
  14. Close the black water valve.
  15. Disconnect the sewer hose at the van end, lifting it so any residual liquid drains toward the dump station.
  16. Close the Van outlet
  17. Rinse the sewer hose by running the rinse hose (or your fresh-water hose) into its open end.
  18. Disconnect the sewer hose from the dump station drain.
  19. Rinse the outlet cap and hose fittings
  20. Recap the sewer outlet.
  21. Clean/rinse everything.
  22. Put away the dirty water hardware & hoses

Clean water
  1. Connect the Fresh water hose
  2. Fill the fresh water tank
  3. Put away the fresh water hose
  4. Close all water ports on the van
  5. Validate everything
